How it works

Three minutes from drawer to server.

01

Install the APK

Sideload a single 19.3 MB APK. No Play Store, no Play Services, no root.

adb install tapbot-v2.2.0.apk
02

Paste your bot token

Message @BotFather on Telegram, create a bot, copy the token, paste it in TapBot.

/newbot → copy token → paste
03

Tap Start

TapBot launches a foreground service on Android ART. Your bot is live. Put the phone in a drawer.

Features

Everything a VPS gives you, in your pocket.

TapStream Music

Stream any song via yt-dlp or tune into 6 curated 24/7 radio stations. Audio routes to the phone speaker, Bluetooth, Telegram in-chat player, or all at once.

yt-dlp320 kbpsSpeaker + Chat

Keystore Encryption

Bot tokens stored in Android Keystore with AES-256-GCM. Never touches disk in plaintext.

Zero Flash Wear

In-memory ring buffers for all logs. Zero bytes written per hour. Your phone storage lasts forever.

Native ART Runtime

Compiled Kotlin bytecode runs directly on Android ART. No interpreter overhead, no Node, no Python.

Background Immortality

Foreground service + wake lock + battery exemption. Survives Doze, app killers, and restarts.
